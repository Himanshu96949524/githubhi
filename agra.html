Taj Mahal & Agra Fort

The Taj Mahal is a symbol of eternal love, built by Emperor Shah Jahan. Nearby, Agra Fort showcases grand palaces and Mughal engineering.

Highlights: Sunrise at the Taj, guided monument tours, local crafts at Sadar Bazaar.
